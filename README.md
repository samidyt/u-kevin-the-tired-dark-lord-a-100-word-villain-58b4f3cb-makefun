A make.fun bounty entry: one monologue of exactly 100 words, spoken by a
fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

## The entry

Lord Veylan of the Grey Marches receives a visitor in his crumbling hall.
Forty years into a siege of attrition against the capital, his household is
falling apart around him — the steward quit, the cook quit, even the ravens
filed a complaint — and still the plan holds: freeze the northern passes,
turn back the grain ships, let the capital eat itself by spring. He only has
to hold the door for one more winter.

The tiredness lives in the pauses, the trailing thoughts and the small
deflections — never in a plain statement of weariness. The sting lands in
the last line: after forty years of doing the pouring himself, tonight
someone else pours.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (100 words, 541
characters — inside the 1–600 character gate).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
